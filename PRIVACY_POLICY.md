# Privacy Policy: Walnex Restyle

**Publisher:** Walnex Technologies
**Last updated:** October 6, 2026
**Contact:** muhammadwalid200@gmail.com

## Summary

Walnex Restyle does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension does

Walnex Restyle changes the appearance of chatgpt.com in your own browser (colours, fonts, spacing, layout) using CSS that it generates from your settings.

## Data we collect

None. The extension makes no network requests, contains no analytics or tracking, and has no user accounts. Walnex Technologies never receives any information from your use of the extension.

## Data stored on your device

Your customization choices (for example selected colours, font, layout options, optional wordmark text and optional custom CSS) are saved using the browser's `chrome.storage.sync` API. This data stays within your browser profile and may be synchronized between your own devices by your browser vendor (for example Google, if you have Chrome Sync enabled), under that vendor's privacy policy. Walnex Technologies cannot access it.

## Access to web pages

The extension runs only on `https://chatgpt.com/*`. It does not read, copy, store or transmit your conversations or any page content. It only adds style rules and, if you choose, changes how the site's name is displayed on your screen.

## Permissions

- **storage**: to save your settings.
- **Content script on chatgpt.com**: to apply your chosen styling to the page.

## Third parties

No third-party services, SDKs or remote code are used.

## Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
